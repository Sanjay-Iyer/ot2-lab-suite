EXPERIMENT: Standard SERS paper printing - nanoparticle and crystal violet series
  experiment_id : experiment_01_standard_printing
  job_id        : 2d290f38abca6a6a8e1c908e1d643e29d16a5db7c2c4c81a86570fdd3ae78a49
  plan_id       : 6f2c68045a956b67cd004f3115368691ee631ff0714e80261298a9d9d70b39f3
  description   : Four printed columns on one 96-position paper substrate. Column 1 pairs an eight-point twofold nanoparticle series with stock crystal violet. Column 2 repeats the nanoparticle deposition three times per position with five minutes between layers before the same stock crystal violet. Column 3 is a matched stock-crystal-violet-only control column. Column 4 is an eight-point twofold crystal violet dilution series with no nanoparticles.
